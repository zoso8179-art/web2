SELF-ASSESSMENT

Where does your sales engine actually stand?

Thanks for giving this some of your day. Set aside up to half an hour.

I built this because most business owners have a feeling about where the gaps sit in their sales engine, but rarely get to see it laid out plainly. Thirty questions, six areas: forecasting, your systems, your process, your commercial foundations, where you sit in your market, and pricing.

Answer honestly. There's nothing to get right or wrong here, just what's actually true for your business.

Each question gives you three ways to answer, plus a box underneath for extra detail if you've got it. That box is optional, but the more you give, the sharper the picture you get back. The quickest way to fill it is to talk rather than type: use the microphone on your keyboard, and give it a quick read afterwards.

At the end you'll see the whole picture in one place, a wheel showing where you're strong and where the real pinch points sit. Take it and go, no strings either way. Hopefully it's worth the time it took.
