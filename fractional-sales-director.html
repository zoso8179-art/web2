What a fractional sales director does

A fractional sales director does the job of a sales director part time, for as long as you need it, without you making a full time senior hire. In practice, I work alongside you and your team for an agreed number of days and take responsibility for how your sales work.

Most of the businesses I talk to have good people working hard. What's usually missing is a sales process everyone understands and follows, a pipeline you can trust, and a forecast you can plan around. That's what I build, with your team, until it runs without me.

When it makes sense

  • You built the business by selling it yourself, and every deal that matters still comes through you.
  • You're about to invest in sales, a senior hire or a bigger team, and want the engine right first.
  • You have a sales team, but the number still surprises you.
  • You have a sales leader and want experienced support alongside them. I work with them, not around them.

What you get

Three foundations of predictable sales, built for your business and run by your team.

How you sell, documented

The stages a deal goes through, what each one means, and who owns it. Everyone on the team works from the same page.

A pipeline you can trust

Which deals are real, which to drop, and a weekly review that runs without you in the room.

A predictable forecast

A forecast built from the deals that are real, and clarity on the actions needed to deliver the number.

How it works

  1. A free conversation about how you sell today: how a deal gets from first conversation to signed, who decides what, and where things go quiet.
  2. The Sales Maturity Self-Assessment, if it helps. Thirty questions across six areas, free, and you keep the results.
  3. We agree what's needed: how many days, for how long, and the fee, before any paid work starts.

Near enough. The titles differ more than the work. I use fractional sales director because it's the term most UK businesses use.
