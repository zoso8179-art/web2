The Problem

Some months your revenue is strong and some months it's thin, and nobody can quite say why. You can't tell whether next quarter's number is safe, so every decision to hire, spend or grow feels like a gamble. Or the business is growing so fast that you're starting to wonder whether the way you sell can carry the weight.

About Me

I've spent twenty years in B2B sales. I've carried a number myself, led sales teams, and run a regional business. That means I've been on both sides of the forecast call, as the person giving the number and the person relying on it.

What I've seen is that unpredictable sales is rarely down to people not working hard. More often, everyone describes a deal in a slightly different way, so nobody is looking at the same picture.

I'd like to understand how it works in your business. I'll ask a lot of questions.
